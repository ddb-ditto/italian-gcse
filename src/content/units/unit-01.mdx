<Section kicker={"Before you start"}>What you need, and why this unit exists</Section>

<p>A notebook and a pen for each child, something that records audio — a phone is fine — and these
pages. No textbook, no worksheets, nothing to buy.</p>

<p>Italian spelling maps onto sound almost perfectly. Unlike French or English, once you know about a
dozen rules you can produce the right sounds for any written word on sight, forever — where the
stress falls is the one thing spelling does not always tell you, which is Session&nbsp;4. That is a
genuinely
unusual gift, and it is worth five sessions at the very start, because everything afterwards —
vocabulary, listening, speaking — gets easier if the sound system is already in place.</p>

<Notebook lead={"A notebook each"}>
<p>They write as well as speak, from the first session. It is not a substitute for saying things
  aloud — it is how a rule stops being something you told them and becomes something they own, and it is
  Italian handwriting practice, which is examined in two of the four papers: all of Paper 4, and Section B of Paper 3, where the questions and the answers are both in Italian.</p>
  <p><strong>Leave the first four pages empty.</strong> The sound rules from this unit — the vowel
  table, the hard and soft rule, the stress rule — get copied there, and stay findable for two years.
  Everything after that runs in order, with every entry dated.</p>
  <p>Each session says what goes in the notebook. Where a word is written, it is said aloud at the same
  time. Silent copying is handwriting practice and nothing else.</p>
</Notebook>

<Note lead={"Hold the line on this"}>
<p>Do <strong>not</strong> teach the meaning of any word in this unit beyond the greetings in
  Session 5. If they are decoding meaning, they are not listening to sound.</p>
</Note>

<Note lead={"Your own preparation"}>
<p>You need to be able to make these sounds too. If you are unsure of any of them, look the word up
  on Forvo or Google Translate's audio before the session. Your pronunciation is the model they will
  copy, so it is worth five minutes.</p>
</Note>
