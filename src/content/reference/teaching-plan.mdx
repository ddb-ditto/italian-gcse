<Section kicker={"Section 1"}>What the qualification actually asks for</Section>

<p>Four papers, 25% each, all taken at the same tier — Foundation or Higher. No dictionaries in any
paper, including speaking preparation time.</p>

<div class="paper">
  <h4>Paper 1 — Listening</h4>
  <p class="timing">Written exam · Foundation 35 min, Higher 45 min (both include 5 min reading time) · 50 marks</p>
  <p>Each passage is played twice. One section is set in English, one in Italian; the order of those two
  sections differs between tiers.</p>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <h4>Paper 2 — Speaking</h4>
  <p class="timing">Conducted by the centre in an April/May window, recorded, marked externally by Pearson ·
  Foundation 7–9 min, Higher 10–12 min, plus 12 min supervised prep · 70 marks</p>
  <p>Three tasks in fixed order:</p>
  <div class="task">
    <span class="tname">Task 1 · Role play — 10 marks</span>
    <p>Formal or informal register as set. Five prompts. Foundation: ask one question, handle one
    unpredictable question. Higher: ask two questions, handle one unpredictable question, plus one
    answer in a past tense.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="task">
    <span class="tname">Task 2 · Picture-based task — 24 marks</span>
    <p>Five questions; the first is about the picture, the rest widen to the topic. Must cover past,
    present and future at <strong>both</strong> tiers.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="task">
    <span class="tname">Task 3 · Conversation — 36 marks</span>
    <p>Two themes. The candidate picks the topic for the first part, agreed at least two weeks before;
    Pearson allocates the second theme. Notes from prep time are handed back before this task starts.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <h4>Paper 3 — Reading</h4>
  <p class="timing">Foundation 45 min, Higher 1 hour · 50 marks, 10 questions</p>
  <p>Three sections: one in English, one in Italian, one translation from Italian into English. Text
  types include adverts, emails, letters, articles and short literary extracts.</p>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <h4>Paper 4 — Writing</h4>
  <p class="timing">Foundation 1 hr 15, Higher 1 hr 20 · 60 marks</p>
  <p><strong>Foundation:</strong> three open questions, roughly 20–30, 40–50 and 80–90 words, plus five
  sentences to translate into Italian.<br />
  <strong>Higher:</strong> two open questions, 80–90 and 130–150 words, plus a paragraph to translate
  into Italian.</p>
  <p>The 80–90 word informal question is common to both tiers.</p>
</div>

<Warn lead={"Register is examined explicitly"}>
<p>Some questions demand formal, some informal. Getting it wrong caps the mark regardless of how good
  the Italian is.</p>
</Warn>

<h3 id="themes">The five themes</h3>

<p>All five must be studied in both a UK and an Italian-speaking context.</p>

<div class="spec">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="badges"><span class="rowlbl">Theme 1</span></div>
    <div><strong>Identity and culture</strong>
      <span class="sub">Relationships, family and friends, interests, role models; daily life, food and
      drink, shopping, social media and technology; celebrations and festivals, reading, music, sport,
      film and TV.</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <div class="badges"><span class="rowlbl">Theme 2</span></div>
    <div><strong>Local area, holiday and travel</strong>
      <span class="sub">Holiday preferences, experiences and destinations; travel, accommodation,
      directions, eating out, shopping, dealing with problems; weather, places to see, things to do.</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <div class="badges"><span class="rowlbl">Theme 3</span></div>
    <div><strong>School</strong>
      <span class="sub">School types, the school day, subjects, rules and pressures, celebrating

  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <div class="badges"><span class="rowlbl">Theme 4</span></div>
    <div><strong>Future aspirations, study and work</strong>
      <span class="sub">Using languages beyond the classroom, further study, volunteering, training,

  </div>
  <div class="row">

<p>Role plays are drawn from themes 1–4 only. The global theme appears in the picture task and
conversation instead.</p>

<Section kicker={"Section 2"}>The shape of the two years</Section>

<p>The spec is deliberately light on content, so there is room for revision at the end. The plan below
front-loads grammar in year one, because grammar is what limits the top bands in every mark grid, then
spends year two turning that into exam performance.</p>

<h3>Year one — build the machinery</h3>

<div class="timeline">
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">Autumn 2026</div>
    <div><strong>Foundations.</strong> Pronunciation, articles and gender, plural forms, present tense
    (-are / -ere / -ire, plus <em>essere</em>, <em>avere</em>, <em>andare</em>, <em>fare</em>), adjective
    agreement, question forms, numbers, time. Theme 1: Who am I? and Daily life.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">Spring 2027</div>
    <div><strong>The past arrives.</strong> Passato prossimo with both auxiliaries and agreement rules,
    reflexives, <em>piacere</em> properly, prepositions and articulated prepositions, direct object
    pronouns. Theme 2 in full — the richest theme for role play practice, so start role plays here.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">Summer 2027</div>
    <div><strong>Second past, and the future.</strong> Imperfect and the imperfect/perfect contrast,
    future tense, comparatives and superlatives, modal verbs. Theme 3, plus a first pass at Theme 1
    cultural life. First full mock: Reading and Listening, Foundation level.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<h3>Year two — turn it into marks</h3>

<div class="timeline">
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">Autumn 2027</div>
    <div>Conditional, indirect object and combined pronouns, <em>ne</em> and <em>ci</em>, relative
    pronouns, impersonal <em>si</em>. Themes 4 and 5. Weekly translation both directions.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">Spring 2028</div>
    <div>Subjunctive (Higher), passive, pluperfect, reported speech. Tier decision made by half term at
    the latest. Speaking drilled hard — this is the April window. Full past papers under timed
    conditions.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">Apr/May 2028</div>
    <div>Speaking assessment at the centre.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="when">
    <div class="date">May/Jun 2028</div>
    <div>Papers 1, 3 and 4.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<Section kicker={"Section 3"}>Weekly rhythm</Section>

<p class="lede">The part that actually matters. Little and often beats long sessions.</p>

<p>Suggested five-day pattern, around 45 minutes a day, adjusted per child:</p>

<WordTable words={[{"term": "Monday", "gloss": "New grammar point, plus drills"}, {"term": "Tuesday", "gloss": "Vocabulary for the current topic — active recall, not reading"}, {"term": "Wednesday", "gloss": "Listening — authentic audio, then exam-style"}, {"term": "Thursday", "gloss": "Writing — one short piece, marked against the actual grids"}, {"term": "Friday", "gloss": "Speaking — role play or picture task, recorded"}]} />

<p>Plus, all week and not counted as lessons: Italian TV, music, and a fifteen-minute conversation at
some fixed point in the day.</p>

<h3>Three habits that pay off disproportionately</h3>

<Plain>
<li><strong>Record every speaking practice from week one.</strong> Spontaneity and repair strategies are explicitly rewarded, and both improve only by doing them badly for a while first.</li>
  <li><strong>Translate both directions every week</strong>, from early on. Two of the four papers contain a translation, and the writing translation is where grammar knowledge converts directly into marks.</li>
  <li><strong>Mark against the real grids</strong>, not a gut feeling. The descriptors are published; use them from the first term so the standard is never a surprise.</li>
</Plain>

<Section kicker={"Section 4"}>Tier decision</Section>

<p>Foundation caps at grade 5. Higher runs 4–9. Each child takes all four papers at one tier, but the
two children do not have to match each other.</p>

<p>Decide by February 2028, based on mock evidence, not optimism.</p>

<Warn lead={"The honest test for Higher"}>
<p>Can they refer to past, present and future accurately and unprompted in speech? Higher tier
  speaking and writing grids reward manipulation of complex structures, and a candidate pushed up a
  tier too early loses marks across all four papers at once, not just one.</p>
</Warn>
